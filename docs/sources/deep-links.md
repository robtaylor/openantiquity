# Deep links

This page lists link templates for opening the same place or record in an external viewer, as the research pass of 2026-11-10 found them. A deep link is what [Decision 0007](../architecture/decisions/0007-the-record-index-stores-only-what-licences-allow.md) calls the `deeplink` access mode: Decision 0007 proposes that Open Antiquity show a link and hold nothing else. The hosts behind the largest record systems, Historic England, the Portable Antiquities Scheme, trove.scot and the Archaeology Data Service, returned a bot challenge to every scripted request. Their templates were read from archived pages and publishers' own links, so most are marked "not tested" and need one check in a real browser.

`GET /context/links` serves every link on this page except the four in `EXCLUDED_LINKS` (`backend/tests/records/context_data.py`), which the research said a browser must check first. The "Verified" column records what the research saw on 2026-11-10 and means the following.

- **yes**: the page loaded with HTTP 200 from a script or in a real browser on that date. The Notes column says which.
- **redirect only**: the host answered a script on that date with a redirect to the new address, but the final page was blocked or not followed.
- **not tested**: the research did not load the template on that date. The Notes column gives any older evidence, such as an archived capture with its date, or says that the pattern was read from page source or memory.

The "Shows" column says what each viewer shows at a place: records, historic maps, aerial photographs, LIDAR, photographs, or other, such as a report or a historical gazetteer. `GET /context/links` gives it as `shows`, from the `shows` field of `worklists/deep-links.json`.

Placeholders in braces are filled from the place or record. `{lat}` and `{lon}` are WGS84 degrees. `{easting}` and `{northing}` are British National Grid, EPSG:27700, in metres, unless the name says `_3857`. `{zoom}` is the viewer's own zoom scale, which differs between viewers. `{id}` is a record identifier.

The viewers use three coordinate systems for one point: WGS84, EPSG:3857 and EPSG:27700. Avebury was the research's test point. It is about E410132 N170023, estimated from a nearby Britain from Above record, and -206408.60, 6697461.16 in EPSG:3857. Latitude and longitude for the same point were -1.8542, 51.4286.

## Hosts that must not be framed or fetched server-side

These hosts returned a challenge or refusal to scripts on 2026-11-10, or their terms forbid framing, scraping or both. The research did not spoof a browser or work round any challenge. A Google Cloud backend therefore cannot check these hosts, and a link's final page can only be checked in a real browser.

## Open questions

- Will Historic England publish a lookup from legacy HOB numbers to the new Research Record GUIDs? Without one, a Research Record link built from a HOB number lands on a text search that may return none or several records.
- Does the Heritage Gateway location link accept a radius or zoom, and does it work without the conditions-of-use click-through?
- Does a JSON API sit behind the new Heritage Gateway results page, and with what parameters, page size and authentication?
- Do the PAS record link, the PAS distance search and the Historic England Archive image record links hold for the patterns above? The PAS terms name the home page only.
- Does the Aerial Photo Explorer or the NHLE map search accept a location in the URL?
- Where does trove.scot/c14index/{id}/ land, and is there a per-place radiocarbon view to link to?
- Does trove.scot now state licence descriptions where clause 6.1 had a placeholder, and have its terms changed since 15 July 2024?
- What are the names of the Heritage Gateway datasets behind resourceIDs 5 and 801 and the other mapped ids?
- What are the record page patterns for Northern Ireland industrial heritage and defence heritage, and for Scottish council HERs other than Highland? None was found.
- Do the pending HER datasets on Heritage Gateway resolve through the legacy redirect yet? For 24 HERs with no site of their own tested, links worked only through the legacy redirects and the record might not load until the dataset was onboarded.
